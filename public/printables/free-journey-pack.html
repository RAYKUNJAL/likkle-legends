How to use this pack

  • Print letter-size pages. Black-and-white is intentional and easy on ink.
  • Read before a new routine, or after, as a gentle debrief.
  • Pause on sensory pages. Your child can point, tap a board, or skip.
  • Celebrate any coping choice — headphones, break, comfort item, words.
  • Ages ~3–5 with adult reading; ~6–9 shared reading.

Story 1 of 3 · Trying a new food

Title

R.O.T.I. Tries Something New

Island kitchen · look, smell, choose

R.O.T.I. Tries Something New

Intro

R.O.T.I. sits at the table with Tanty Spice. A new plate is waiting. The food smells warm and spicy.

Journey Story · page 2 of 5

Sensory

The plate is close. Steam rises. The food looks orange and soft. R.O.T.I. can look first. Looking is enough.

Journey Story · page 3 of 5

Coping

R.O.T.I. can smell the food. R.O.T.I. can touch it with a spoon. R.O.T.I. can take a tiny taste, or put the spoon down. Choosing is okay.

Journey Story · page 4 of 5

Conclusion

R.O.T.I. tried something new in a small way. Tanty Spice is proud. Trying at your own pace is brave.

Story 2 of 3 · Loud fête

Title

Steelpan Sam at the Loud Fête

Carnival energy · headphones & quiet corners

Steelpan Sam at the Loud Fête

Intro

Steelpan Sam and Mango Moko walk toward the fête. Music is playing. People are smiling and moving.

Journey Story · page 2 of 5

Sensory

The steelpan rings bright and loud. Drums boom. Voices cheer. The ground feels busy under their feet.

Journey Story · page 3 of 5

Coping

Sam can put on soft headphones. Sam can step to a quieter corner. Sam can watch from the edge. Taking a break is okay.

Journey Story · page 4 of 5

Conclusion

Sam enjoyed the fête in a way that felt safe. Loud joy can share space with quiet moments. Sam did it.

Story 3 of 3 · Haircut day

Title

Tanty and the Haircut Day

Salon / barber · snips, mist, breaks

Tanty and the Haircut Day

Intro

Today is haircut day. Tanty Spice walks with a friend to the chair. The mirror is shiny. The cape is soft.

Journey Story · page 2 of 5

Sensory

The cape rests on shoulders. Scissors make a snip-snip sound. A spray bottle mists cool water. The chair can spin a little.

Journey Story · page 3 of 5

Coping

You can hold a comfort item. You can ask for a break. You can look at a picture while the clippers buzz, or wait until they stop.

Journey Story · page 4 of 5

Conclusion

The haircut is done. The mirror shows a fresh look. You were calm and brave. Hair grows back. You are still you.
